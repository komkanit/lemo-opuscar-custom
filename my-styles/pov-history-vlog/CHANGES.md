# POV History Vlog · POV vlog ย้อนยุค — decisions

Made from a blank page. "Base" is the technique base, Manga Panels (`manga-panels`), or "—" for something new.

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | a black-and-white manga page that moves, panels as cuts | a person of the past vlogs their own day; one message, surprising sourced facts, things "from the future" marked | the user wants history through a POV nobody has seen, with facts that make people say "wow" |
| §1 Spine | — | one sentence first; plant early, pay off at the end; the ending follows the message | v1 was "loose": a day of facts and an unrelated ending ("how many days would you last?"); the user rejected it |
| §2 Materials | G-pen ink and screentone printed from generated art | photoreal smartphone-vlog stills (selfie / first person), one chat with references; the phone in code: clamped camera, shake, punch-in | "real but like a phone vlog"; ChatGPT through Chrome works for the user |
| §2 Archive | — | the same still as a sepia photograph for the payoff | the climax "4,500 years later" needed the picture to age |
| §2 Size | 1920×1080 | 1080×1920 | Shorts, TikTok, Reels |
| §3 Colour | ink, paper, one vermilion | natural colour of the stills; white = said now, yellow = added / from the future, red = REC and the shock | vlog editing conventions, one meaning per colour |
| §4 Type | manga lettering and balloons | Kanit Black captions with outline at 66.5 %; POV hook card; vlog UI (REC, DAY 1, clock, pin); Mali stickers and arrows; yellow future tag and end card | short-video grammar; the hook moved to the chest after it covered the face; no "1 day in the life of…" card (another channel's template) |
| §5 Motion | panel reveals, speed lines | slow inside shots, snappy between: jump-cut snap, flash, punch-in, pops, shock shake, future glitch, white loop | phone vlog editing |
| §6 Camera | panels as shots | the vlogger's own camera; punch-in instead of close-up cuts; new shot = new hour or place | POV is the point |
| §7 Sound | — (manga demo score) | first-person ElevenLabs voice leading by ≥ 6 dB; hand percussion and harp of the place; silence on the shock; chime swell on future facts; room tone per shot | voice-first like the user's other series |
| §8 Native moves | panel break, speed-line hit | the date jump (signature); plant and pay off; punch-box-number; shock cut; archive reveal | the user asked for the clock to mean more; the date jump carries the message across time |
| Language | — | one language per channel; Thai voice and captions, English as `.srt` | the user's decision: Thai channel first, never mix languages on one channel |
