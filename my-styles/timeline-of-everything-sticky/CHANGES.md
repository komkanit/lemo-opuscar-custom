# Timeline of Everything · Sticky Figure — decisions

Made from a blank page. "Base" is the technique base, Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (`scifi-toon`), or "—" for something new.

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | dialogue-driven adult sitcom, a duo, worlds via a portal | one walker walks in place on a timeline road; the world scrolls right to left and changes era by era | the user's format: "the timeline of everything" told by one stick figure |
| §2 Line | ~7 px boiling outline, 3 drawings at 12 fps | constant 5.5 / 4.5 / 3.5 px clean ink, no boil; far layers outlined in their own darker shade | the user's references are clean vector stick figures |
| §2 Characters | big white eyes, elastic mouths, noodle limbs, absurd costumes | white circle head, dot eyes, one-line mouth, single-line body and limbs, white mitten hands; flat clothes put on the stick; men and women | the user's character references; mismatches resolved as recommended (white mittens, simple faces) |
| §2 Clothes | one costume per lead | one outfit per era for the walker; hair and face never change | "clothes change with the era of the content" |
| §2 Depth | flat sets | depth factor `k`: size, height on the ground and scroll speed share one number | makes the side-scroll read as a place; keeps scale honest |
| §2 Frames | 1920×1080 | 1920×1080 and 1080×1920 from the same timeline, each with its own anchors | the user posts to YouTube and TikTok/Reels |
| §3 Colour | one palette per world, maximally different | one soft palette per era; ink, heads and paper fixed; the walker's clothes the most saturated | the eras must read at a glance while the walker stays the anchor |
| §4 Type | Baloo 2 subtitles with speaker pills, VT323 tags | Mali (Thai + Latin) for everything; a date card in the sky and a caption box on the ground, from the user's sketch | Thai and English versions; the user's layout sketch |
| §5 Motion | characters on twos (12 fps) | 30 fps on ones; one step per beat; planted feet locked to the road | the road and the foot must move together on every frame |
| §6 Camera | two-shots, reaction close-ups, portal rush zooms | locked to the walker; push on stops; tilt up; scale check; map view; speed-up | a journey along a timeline, with the atlas's road as the overview |
| §7 Sound | theremin sci-fi score, dialogue, burps of foley | acoustic score at walking tempo with an era colour; footsteps per surface; chime per era; card ticks; one narrator | the walk is the beat; a calm explainer |
| §8 Native moves | portal worlds, duo contrast, rule of three | treadmill of time, the era line, rolling date card (signature); stop-point-call-out, map view, scale check, lend a hand | — (new) |
| §10 Engine | toon.js, chars.js, worlds.js | its own rig (two-bone legs with planted feet), outfits, depth-scrolled world and cards, with scifi-toon's constant-width drawing and pixel-rpg's parallax as techniques | the look is the user's, not the base's |
